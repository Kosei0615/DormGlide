How it works

Three steps, no checkout

1

Request or Book

Tap Request Purchase on an item, or Book on a service. The other student accepts.

2

Meet on campus

Agree on a public spot and time in chat. Inspect the item, or show up for the session.

3

Pay at pickup

Cash or a payment app, directly to the other student, right there.

💡 The one rule: Pay at pickup, after you've seen it, never before. DormGlide never handles your money.
