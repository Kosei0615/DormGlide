Welcome to DormGlide, a marketplace where students at supported universities buy and sell used goods with other students on their own campus. By creating an account or using DormGlide, you agree to these Terms.

1. Who can use DormGlide

You must sign up with a school email address from a supported university and confirm it. Your account belongs to your campus market: you see, and are seen by, students at your school only. You must be at least 17 years old and affiliated with your school.

2. Your account

You are responsible for your account and what happens on it. Keep your password to yourself. One person, one account. We may suspend accounts that misrepresent identity, harass others, or abuse the platform.

3. Listings and conduct

List only items you own and can hand over. No prohibited items (weapons, alcohol, drugs, stolen goods, counterfeits, anything illegal or against your school's code of conduct). Photos and descriptions must be of the actual item. Be honest about condition. Treat other students the way you'd want to be treated in the dining hall.

4. Payments — DormGlide never handles money

DormGlide does not process, hold, or transfer money. All payment happens directly between buyer and seller, in person at handoff, using cash or a peer-to-peer app (such as Venmo, Zelle, or Cash App) that the two of you choose. DormGlide is not a party to your transaction, takes no fees from it, and cannot refund, reverse, or guarantee any payment. The rule we recommend and repeat throughout the app: pay at pickup, after you've seen the item, never before.

5. Meetups and reservations

Deals complete in person on or near campus. Meet in public campus locations. A reservation on a reserve-ahead listing is a mutual commitment, not a contract of sale: either side may cancel before handoff, and no money is owed for a cancelled reservation. Repeated no-shows may affect your seller rating and account standing.

6. Your content

You keep ownership of the photos and text you post. You give DormGlide permission to display them to students on your campus so the marketplace can work. Don't post content you don't have the right to share.

7. Ending your account

You can stop using DormGlide at any time and request account deletion. We may suspend or terminate accounts that violate these Terms.

10. Services (Glyde)

Glyde lets students offer services — tutoring, hair and beauty, tech help, photography, moving help, pet sitting, fitness coaching, music lessons — to other students on their campus. A student offering a service ("provider") is an independent individual, not an employee, contractor, or agent of DormGlide.
