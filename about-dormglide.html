DormGlide
The campus marketplace for Denison students · dormglide.com

DormGlide is a free, campus-only app where Denison students buy and sell the stuff of dorm life, book each other's skills, and get things delivered across campus. Every account is a verified @denison.edu email, you only see your own campus, and people pay each other in person. The app never touches money.

Built to fit campus rules

  • Verified and local. Sign-up requires a @denison.edu address confirmed by email. Students see, and are seen by, their own campus only.
  • Housing policy built in. Listings for items prohibited in the residence halls (AC units, microwaves, candles, space heaters, lofts, personal mattresses, and more) are blocked at the form and hidden automatically if they slip through. Service posts show the shared-space rules first, and no one may run a shop out of a hall room.
  • Safe by design. No money in the app, ratings after every deal, in-app chat, and a clear list of services that are not allowed (anything involving minors, medical care, licensed work, ghostwriting).

How it works

  • 1. Sign up with your @denison.edu email. One minute, no app store.
  • 2. Post or browse. Items on one side, skills on the other. Flip the switch at the top.
  • 3. Chat and meet. Agree on a time in chat, meet on campus, pay in person after you've seen it.

Made and run by Denison students. Founded by Kosei Yamaguchi '28 and built with a small team of Denisonians. We got tired of hauling a mini fridge home in May and paying full price for a lamp in August, so we built the fix. DormGlide is a student project, not a university service.
