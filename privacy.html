DormGlide Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 · Effective September 2026

DRAFT — pending legal review. Not yet reviewed by an attorney.

What we collect

  • Your school email and display name — to verify you're a student and show who you're dealing with.
  • Your listings and photos — the items you post, visible to students on your campus.
  • Messages — conversations between you and other students about listings.
  • Ratings — the seller ratings you give and receive.
  • Wishlist alerts and notifications — what you tell us you're looking for, and the notifications we send you about it.
  • Deal activity — purchase requests, meetup notes, and deal status, shared between you and your counterparty.

What we do with it

We use this information for one thing: running your campus marketplace. Showing your listings to your campus, delivering your messages, matching wishlists to new listings, and sending you the notifications and emails you'd expect (confirmations, wishlist matches, handoff reminders).

What we never do

We do not sell your data. We don't show ads, we don't share your information with data brokers, and your activity is visible only within your own campus market.

Who processes data for us

  • Supabase — hosts our database, accounts, and photo storage.
  • Resend — delivers our emails (confirmations, wishlist matches, reminders).

Both act on our instructions to run the service.

Payment information

We never see it. DormGlide doesn't process payments — you pay each other directly in person. Payment app usernames you share in chat are messages between you and the other student, not payment data we handle.

Your choices

You can edit your profile, delete your listings and alerts, and request deletion of your account and its data through the app's support flow. Deleting your account removes your profile, listings, photos, and alerts.

Changes

If this policy changes meaningfully, we'll tell you in the app before the change applies to you.
